DONT USE /FILL!!!!!!

regular commands like /fill are bad because you need to put coords and you cant undo. I stopped using them a looong time ago when i mistyped a coord with /fill and destroyed like weeks worth of work in 2 seconds and it was impossible to undo it.

worldedit is easier. all worldedit commands start with // instead of / so they stand out.

if u wanna choose coords, go in spectator or creative mode and type //pos1 while standing in one corner and //pos2 while standing in the other. you can also choose pos1 and pos2 by left and right clicking on a block while holding a wooden axe.

you can do many commands with // but the most important ones are:

  • //set - the same thing as /fill
  • //replace - replace a certian block with another
  • //copy and //paste - self explainatory. these will copy and paste in relation to where you are standing to the selection
  • //undo - oopsie
